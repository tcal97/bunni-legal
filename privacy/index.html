bunni is an iPhone app that shows your net worth by reading balances and holdings from accounts you choose to connect. This policy explains what data bunni handles, why, where it is kept, how long, and your rights. bunni is read-only: it cannot move money, make payments or place trades.

2. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): providing the service you asked for, which means showing your own financial data back to you.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): connecting each financial account is your choice, and so is each Ask bunni request. You can withdraw consent at any time by disconnecting the account, not using Ask bunni, or deleting your account (see section 6).

3. How we use your data

Only to show you your own finances inside bunni and its widgets: totals, history, breakdowns, reminders, comparisons and goals. We do not sell, rent or share your data. We don't use it for advertising or credit decisions, and neither we nor our processors use it to train AI models.

Ask bunni (AI)

Ask bunni is optional and only runs when you tap it ("See what bunni thinks" or "Ask bunni for a plan"). The first time, bunni explains what will be sent and asks for your agreement. Each time you ask, your iPhone sends a summary to our server, which passes it to Anthropic (Claude) to write bunni's answer. The summary contains your totals by category, your accounts by type and institution, your holdings with their values and recent changes, your monthly net-worth history, your goals, and the age, country, area and pay you added. It never contains your name, email address, account numbers or transactions.

We don't store the summary or the answer on our servers; the answer is saved on your iPhone. Anthropic processes it as our processor under its commercial terms, keeps it only for a limited period as described in its privacy policy, and doesn't use it to train its models. bunni's answers are general information, not financial advice. bunni doesn't make automated decisions about you.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or better) and at rest. Each user can only access their own data, which is enforced in the database. bunni is protected by Face ID / passcode, and a fresh check is required before any bank is linked. See the bunni Information Security Policy for details.

6. Retention and deletion

  • While your account is open, we keep the data needed to show your dashboard.
  • Disconnecting an account in bunni ends that bank connection at Enable Banking (or revokes it at Plaid) and deletes its stored data. Bank consent also expires by itself after at most 180 days.
  • Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account and data) immediately ends every bank connection (Enable Banking and Plaid) connection and permanently deletes your account and all associated data from our database. It also clears the data bunni stored on your iPhone, including Keychain keys.
  • Mortgage pack answers and documents stay on your iPhone, protected by iOS file encryption, until you remove them, tap "Start over", sign out or delete your account. Transactions read for the statement check are never stored.
  • Ask bunni usage records are deleted after 60 days. Details, goals and answers on your iPhone are deleted when you remove them, sign out or delete your account.
  • Encrypted database backups held by Supabase expire within their backup period, at most 30 days.
  • If you can't use the app, email us and we'll delete your data within 30 days.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR you can access, correct, delete or export your data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Most of this you can do directly in the app; for anything else, email tomcallaghan4@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission (Ireland) at dataprotection.ie.

8. Children

bunni is not intended for anyone under 18.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, bunni will ask you to review and accept the new version. The version and date you accepted are recorded with your account.
